# SEA (Sanika Elevation Agency) — Roofing Client Acquisition Game Plan

**Prepared:** Friday, July 17, 2026
**Goal:** 5 qualified leads (booked sales calls with roofing / home-improvement company owners) by Monday, July 27, 2026 — a 10-day sprint.
**Definition of a "lead" for this sprint:** a decision-maker at a roofing or home-improvement company who books and shows up to a discovery call with SEA. Not a form fill, not a "sounds interesting" reply.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Ads alone won't sign agency clients on a 10-day clock.** Roofers get pitched by marketing agencies constantly; cold ads selling "marketing services" are the lowest-trust, slowest channel for an agency's own client acquisition. The agencies that win roofing clients (Hook Agency, Roofing Webmasters, Webrunner, Contractor Dynamics) win on **niche authority + proof + outbound + referrals**, and use ads mostly for retargeting warm audiences.
- **Your target market is huge and fragmented:** ~105,000–109,000 roofing businesses in the US, a ~$92.5B contractor industry where the top 3 companies hold under 4% combined share. ~80% of demand is re-roofing/renovation, and storm activity keeps demand strong. There are thousands of roofers doing $100k–$500k/month ($1.2M–$6M/yr) — big enough to afford $2k–$6k/mo retainers, small enough to have no in-house marketing.
- **The killer pitch is speed-to-lead automation, not "more ads."** 95% of home-service companies fail to respond to a new lead within 5 minutes and 40% never respond at all — yet responding in under 5 minutes makes a lead ~21x more likely to qualify, and 78% of customers buy from whoever responds first. Selling roofers a machine that answers, texts back, and books estimates automatically is a provable, differentiated offer.
- **The 10-day math works on outbound volume, not hope:** at 2026 benchmarks (25–35 dials per booked meeting; 7–14% reply rates on signal-based cold email to contractors; ~30 booked calls per 2–3 closed clients for a new agency), the sprint below is engineered to produce **8–12 booked calls so 5 show up**.

## 3. How $100k–$500k/mo Roofing Companies Actually Sell (Their Playbook)

Understand this cold — it's both your **pitch material** (you're selling them upgrades to this machine) and your **qualification checklist**.

1. **Lead sources, ranked by close rate:**
   - Referrals & repeat customers — highest trust, easiest close; the lifeblood at this size.
   - Google Local Services Ads (LSA) — $40–$120/verified lead (typical $65–$95), ~7.84x closed ROAS; the most cost-efficient paid channel in home services.
   - Google Ads (non-branded search) — ~$124/lead average, $15–$65 CPC in big metros; closes at 30–40% because intent is high.
   - Facebook/Instagram — $25–$75/lead ($15–$40 optimized; storm campaigns $15–$35); cheaper but colder, needs hard follow-up.
   - Door-knocking/canvassing after storms — 15–25% close; labor-heavy.
2. **Sales process:** free inspection → photo documentation → same-day proposal with **good/better/best options** → financing offer → structured follow-up (most roofing jobs don't close on visit one). Typical close rate on inspected jobs: 20–40%. Raising it 5–10 points adds $500k–$1M/yr — which is why sales enablement (CRM, follow-up automation) sells as well as lead gen.
3. **Speed-to-lead discipline (mostly absent):** contacting within 5 minutes = ~21x more likely to qualify, 8x conversion vs 30-minute response; contractors answering in ~2 minutes convert 62% of leads vs 28% at the 42-minute industry average. **95% of companies don't hit 5 minutes; 40% never respond.** This is the single biggest fixable leak in their business — and your easiest proof-of-value.
4. **Budget norms:** 5–15% of revenue on marketing; ~60–70% of it to paid digital. A $250k/mo roofer "should" spend $12k–$37k/mo — most spend far less, badly.
5. **Tools they use:** CRMs like JobNimbus, AccuLynx, ServiceTitan, ProLine, Roofr; review generation; blogging/SEO (companies that blog generate up to 88% more leads).

**Translation into SEA's pitch:** "You're paying $65–$124 per lead and then losing half of them because nobody calls back within 5 minutes. I'll fix the leak first (speed-to-lead automation), then fill the bucket (LSA + Google + storm campaigns)."

## 4. Why Your Current Ads Aren't Producing

Diagnosis based on how this market behaves:

1. **Wrong channel for agency client acquisition.** Cold Meta ads selling agency services to roofers = pitching the most-pitched audience in home services with the least-trusted format. Successful roofing agencies use ads for **retargeting** people who already engaged; net-new clients come from outbound, referrals, content authority, and partnerships.
2. **Generic offer.** "We do marketing / we get you leads" is invisible. Winning offers are specific, quantified, and de-risked: *"10 booked roof-estimate appointments in 30 days or you don't pay."* Niche-focused agencies close at roughly **2x the rate of generalists** — the niche is necessary but not sufficient; the offer must carry risk-reversal.
3. **No proof assets in the path.** Roofers buy case studies with numbers (e.g., Webrunner publicizes "300+ exclusive leads/mo at ~$175–$220 CPL for metal roofs"; Hook Agency leads with 175+ five-star reviews). If SEA's landing page lacks a named case study with numbers, ads convert strangers into skeptics.
4. **Follow-up leak on your own leads.** The same speed-to-lead rule applies to you: any form fill or DM reply must get a call + SMS within 5 minutes. If your ad leads sat for hours, that's where they died.

**Sprint decision:** pause cold-audience ad spend for 10 days (keep $10–20/day retargeting only). Reallocate the budget to data, phone, and email infrastructure below.

## 6. The Offer (Fix This Before Sending Anything)

Lead with a **pilot offer** engineered for a 10-minute yes:

> **"The 5-Minute Roofer" pilot** — In 14 days I'll install a system that answers every lead in under 5 minutes, 24/7 (instant SMS-back on missed calls, auto follow-up, auto booking), and launch one storm/repair campaign. **If it doesn't produce booked estimates in the first 30 days, you don't pay the management fee.** One roofer per market — [City] slot open.

Why this works:
- **Provable pain:** show them their own leak live — call their office number during the demo; if it rings out with no text-back, the pitch makes itself.
- **Risk reversal** answers "we got burned by an agency before" (they all did).
- **Exclusivity + scarcity** ("one roofer per metro") creates urgency honestly.
- **Priced to close fast:** $1,500–$2,500 pilot or hybrid ($1,500 base + $150–$250/booked estimate), upgrading to a $3k–$6k/mo retainer after the pilot proves out. Don't lead with $5k/mo on a cold first client.

**Proof asset if you have zero case studies yet:** run the system free for 7 days for one local roofer you can reach through any warm connection, in exchange for a testimonial + permission to share numbers. One real number beats ten mockups.

## 8. THE 10-DAY SPRINT — July 17–27 (5 booked-and-shown leads)

### The volume math (why this produces 5+)

| Channel | Volume over 10 days | Benchmark | Expected booked calls |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold calls | 400–500 dials (50–60/day × 8 working days) | 25–35 dials/meeting (SMB founders answer; 10–15% connects) | **4–8** |
| Signal-based cold email | 1,500–2,500 sends from Day 3 (150–300/day, pre-warmed) | 7–14% reply on storm/permit/ad-signal emails; ~1 booking per 150–250 sends | **2–5** |
| Personalized DMs + Loom audits | 150 DMs + 30 Looms | ~1 booking per 30–50 quality touches | **2–4** |
| Referrals & partnerships | 20 asks (suppliers, reps, past contacts, adjacent trades) | warm intros close 2–5x cold | **1–3** |
| **Total pipeline** | | | **9–20 booked → 5+ show** |

Expect 30–50% no-show without confirmation automation — that's why reminders are non-negotiable, and why we aim for ~2x the goal.

### Day-by-day

**Day 0 — Fri Jul 17 (today): Foundation.**
- Write the pilot offer (Section 6) into a one-page PDF + a 5-slide deck.
- Buy 2–3 secondary domains (e.g., sea-elevation.com variants) + pre-warmed inboxes; connect to Instantly/Smartlead.
- Stand up GHL: pipeline, booking calendar with SMS reminders, missed-call text-back on your own line (practice what you preach).
- Start the prospect list: Apollo + Maps scrape → target 750–1,000 roofers/home-improvement companies in 3–5 storm metros. Priority tags: "running ads" (check Meta Ad Library), "<50 Google reviews," "no chat/instant response on site."

**Day 1 — Sat Jul 18: Assets.**
- Scripts finalized (Section 9). Record one 90-second "who we are + the 5-minute rule" video.
- Build the SEA Roofing Snapshot in GHL (missed-call text-back + speed-to-lead workflow minimum) — this is your live demo.
- Join 5–8 roofing/contractor Facebook groups; follow 50 target companies on IG/LinkedIn. No pitching yet — comment usefully.

**Day 2 — Sun Jul 19: Load the machine.**
- Segment list into A (100 dream-fit: right size, running ads, weak follow-up), B (300 good-fit), C (rest).
- Personalize first-lines for the A-list (or run Clay). Queue email campaign #1 (storm/leak-leak angle) to start Day 3. Record first 5 Loom audits for A-list.

**Days 3–4 — Mon–Tue Jul 20–21: Launch everything.**
- **Calls:** 50–60 dials/day, 9–11am and 4–6pm local (owners answer early/late). Voicemail + immediate follow-up text each miss.
- **Email:** goes live, 150–300/day across inboxes.
- **DMs:** 20/day (IG/FB/LinkedIn) — comment first, then message.
- **Looms:** 5/day to A-list ("I found 3 leaks in how [Company] handles leads — 3-min video, no pitch").
- **Referral asks:** message every warm contact + 5 supplier/manufacturer territory reps: "$500 for any intro that becomes a client."
- Book everything into the GHL calendar; reminders fire automatically.

**Days 5–6 — Wed–Thu Jul 22–23: Follow-up is the job.**
- Touch 2–3 on all non-answers (a different angle each time — the money is in touches 3–7).
- Ringless voicemail drop (e.g., Slybroadcast) to the called-no-answer list.
- Post one value piece in the FB groups (e.g., "We audited 40 roofers this week — 90% never texted back a missed call. Here's the 5-minute fix"). This converts your outbound into inbound.
- Turn on $10–20/day retargeting to site visitors/engagers only.

**Days 7–8 — Fri–Sat Jul 24–25: Double down on what's working.**
- Check the scoreboard (below). Whichever channel produced bookings gets +50% volume; kill the weakest.
- Re-Loom the 20 warmest non-responders with a sharper angle (their actual ad running in Meta Ad Library + their missed-call test result).
- Confirm Monday/Tuesday calls personally by SMS.

**Days 9–10 — Sun–Mon Jul 26–27: Close the loop.**
- Final-pass sequence to all opens/clicks/partial replies: honest deadline — "Taking 2 pilot roofers for August; picking by Wednesday."
- Re-confirm every booked call (24h + 1h SMS). Rebook any no-show within 10 minutes ("Owner got pulled onto a roof? Happens — here are two slots tomorrow").
- Count: 5+ held calls = goal. Log everything for the next cycle.

## 9. Scripts & Templates

**Cold call opener (owner picks up):**
> "Hey [Name], it's [You] with SEA — I'll be quick because I know you're probably on a roof. We work only with roofing companies, and before calling I tested your lead line: when a call gets missed, nothing texts the homeowner back. 78% of homeowners go with whoever responds first — so that's jobs quietly going to [competitor]. I fix that leak in 14 days, and if it doesn't produce booked estimates in 30, you don't pay. Worth 15 minutes this week to see it on your own numbers?"

**Cold email #1 (signal-based — the style getting 7–14% replies with contractors):**
> **Subject:** missed call at [Company] yesterday
> [Name] — called your office [day] at [time]; rang out, no text-back. Homeowners do the same thing after a storm, then call the next roofer on Google (78% hire whoever answers first).
> We install a system for roofers that texts back in 15 seconds, follows up automatically, and books the estimate — takes 14 days, and if it doesn't produce booked estimates in 30 days, you don't pay.
> Want the 3-minute video of the leak I found on your line? — [You], SEA (we only do roofing & home improvement)

**Cold email #2 (running-ads signal):**
> **Subject:** your Facebook ad ([hook from Meta Ad Library])
> Saw the ad you're running for [service/city] — decent hook. The leak is usually after the click: at $25–$75 a roofing lead, a 40-minute response time converts 28% instead of 62%. That's the same ad spend buying half the jobs.
> We fix response + follow-up first, then scale the ads. Pilot is guaranteed: no booked estimates in 30 days, no fee. Open to 15 minutes Thursday?

**DM (after engaging with their content):**
> "Solid work on the [project] post. Quick one — is [Company] booked out for August, or still filling the schedule? Asking because we only work with roofers and I noticed two quick fixes on how your leads get handled. Happy to send a 3-min video, no strings."

**Loom audit structure (3–5 min):** their missed-call test result → their Google reviews vs top competitor → their ad (if any) + landing page leak → the 2-fix plan → "if you want this installed, the pilot's guaranteed — link below."

**Referral ask:** "I'm taking on 2 more roofing companies in August. $500 for any intro that becomes a client — who's the best roofer you know who's slammed but sloppy with leads?"

## 10. After the Sprint: The 30–90 Day Compounding Engine

1. **Case-study flywheel:** every pilot → a numbers case study within 45 days → outbound proof + FB-group content + retargeting creative.
2. **Email at full scale:** your own domains finish warming ~Aug 7 → 1,000+/day signal-based sends (storm data via HailTrace-style feeds, new-permit lists, Meta Ad Library monitors) — the layered 2026 stack (Apollo/Clay → Instantly/Smartlead) at ~$250–$400/mo.
3. **Authority channel:** weekly short video (audit teardowns, before/afters) on YouTube/LinkedIn/FB groups — this is precisely how Hook Agency and Roofing Insights made outbound optional; niche agencies close at ~2x generalists.
4. **Partnership engine:** formalize supplier/manufacturer-rep referrals (10% or $500 flat), co-host a "5-Minute Roofer" webinar with a distributor branch; explore GAF/Owens Corning contractor-program events and IRE (International Roofing Expo).
5. **Productize delivery:** the GHL snapshot becomes a $297–$497/mo SaaS floor under every retainer (sticky revenue even if ad management churns); upsell LSA management (the 7.84x-ROAS channel) as the first paid-media add-on.
6. **Pricing ladder:** pilot ($1.5k–$2.5k) → hybrid ($2–4k base + $150–$400/booked estimate) → full retainer ($3k–$12k/mo, 90-day KPI exit clause stated up front as a selling point).
7. **Ads, round two — done right:** restart cold ads only after 2–3 case studies exist, pointing at a case-study VSL page with instant SMS follow-up on every form fill (your own 5-minute rule).
