## The 60-second summary

1. **Your ads aren't the fast path to 5 leads — outbound is.** Ads take 2–4 weeks of learning phase and budget to mature. Signal-based outbound (email + Loom audits + calls + DMs) reliably books B2B calls in 10 days. Keep ads running for retargeting only during the sprint.
2. **Lead with a storm hook.** Major hail hit 12 states on June 1, 10 states on June 26, and the Northern Plains + Texas on July 7, 2026. Homeowners in those metros are filing insurance claims *right now* — searches spike 300–800% after a storm, and storm leads close at 45–65% vs 15–25% for normal leads. Roofers in those metros have money on the table and are the easiest to start a conversation with.
3. **Sell one clear offer:** "The Roofing Lead Machine — ads + 30-second speed-to-lead automation. 10 qualified leads in 30 days or you don't pay the management fee."
4. **The numbers:** ~300 targeted roofers, 30 Loom audits, 150 cold calls, 50 DMs over 10 days → 25–40 replies → 8–12 interested → **5+ booked calls**.
5. **Automate everything after the reply:** GoHighLevel pipeline, 30-second speed-to-lead SMS, missed-call text-back, 7-day nurture. This is also *the product you sell*.
