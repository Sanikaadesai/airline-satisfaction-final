# The 10-Day Sprint: 5 Leads by Sunday, July 26

**A "lead" = a booked discovery call with the owner/decision-maker of a roofing or home-improvement company who has a real problem you can solve.** Not a form fill, not a "send me info" — a call on the calendar.

## Why this works: the math

| Activity (10 days) | Volume | Expected response | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold emails (manual, 25–30/day) | ~275 | 7–14% reply w/ signal-based copy | 19–38 replies |
| Loom video audits | 30 | ~34% reply | 10 replies |
| Cold calls | 150 | ~15% connect, ~20% of connects interested | 4–6 interested |
| FB/IG DMs + roofing groups | 50 | 10–20% reply | 5–10 replies |
| Referral/partnership asks | 10 | 1–2 warm intros | 1–2 hot leads |

Pipeline: **~35–55 total replies → 10–15 genuinely interested → 5–8 booked calls.** You only need 5. The model has slack built in.

**Who to target (ICP):** residential roofers doing roughly $1M–$5M/yr in metros hit by the June 1, June 26, or July 7 hail events (TX, OK, KS, MO, NE, CO, MN, and Plains states — verify current maps on [hail.org](https://www.hail.org/) or [HailStrike](https://hailstrike.com/hail-search)). Prioritize roofers **already running ads** (check the [Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/) — free): they already pay for marketing, they just need it done better.

**The offer you're selling on every touch:**
> "10 qualified roofing leads in 30 days or you don't pay the management fee. Exclusive leads + a 30-second speed-to-lead machine installed on your number."

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 — Fri, Jul 17 (today): Build the machine — 3–4 hrs
- [ ] Pick 3–5 target metros from the June/July hail maps.
- [ ] Build the list: 300 roofers (Google Maps/Outscraper + Apollo). Columns: company, owner name, cell/office #, email, website, review count, "running ads? Y/N" (Ad Library), storm metro.
- [ ] Flag the top 50: running ads OR 100+ reviews OR clearly growing. These get Looms.
- [ ] Set up the pipeline in GHL (or a sheet): New → Contacted → Replied → Call Booked → Proposal → Won.
- [ ] Put your booking link in every signature/bio. Test it on your phone.
- [ ] Start warming 2 secondary email domains for August (10 min, pays off later).
- [ ] Send first 25 emails (scripts in file 04) + 10 DMs tonight. **Momentum beats perfection.**

### Day 2 — Sat, Jul 18: Loom day — 3 hrs
- [ ] Record 10 Loom audits on top-50 prospects (checklist in file 04 — 3 min each, don't polish).
- [ ] Send each with the short Loom email/DM.
- [ ] 25 cold emails + 10 DMs.
- [ ] Join 3–5 roofing Facebook groups (Roofing Insights community, storm-restoration groups, local contractor groups). Comment helpfully on 5 posts. **No pitching yet.**

### Day 3 — Sun, Jul 19: Light day — 2 hrs
- [ ] 10 more Looms (running total: 20).
- [ ] 25 emails + 10 DMs.

### Day 4 — Mon, Jul 20: First call blitz — 4 hrs
- [ ] **50 dials**, 8–10am and 4–6pm prospect-local time (owners answer early/late). Script in file 04.
- [ ] Bump #1 email to Days 1–2 non-repliers (the 2-line "bubbling this up" — bumps often out-reply the original).
- [ ] 25 emails to fresh names.
- [ ] **Answer every reply within 5 minutes. Push every warm reply to a booked time, never "sure, anytime."**

### Day 5 — Tue, Jul 21: Compound — 3–4 hrs
- [ ] 50 dials.

- [ ] 25 emails + LinkedIn connects w/ short note to 15 owners.

### Day 6 — Wed, Jul 22: Storm angle hard — 3 hrs
- [ ] Check hail.org for *this week's* storms — email/call roofers in any metro hit in the last 7 days with the storm-surge script ("searches in your metro are spiking 300–800% this week — are you capturing it?").
- [ ] Bump #2 (the P.S.-guarantee bump) to all non-repliers.

- [ ] Mid-sprint scoreboard: replies, interested, booked. If reply rate < 5%: tighten the first line (more specific signal). If replies but no bookings: you're pitching too much in-thread — push to the call sooner.

### Day 7 — Thu, Jul 23: Second call blitz — 4 hrs
- [ ] 50 dials — prioritize everyone who opened twice, watched a Loom, or replied anywhere.

### Day 8 — Fri, Jul 24: Convert warm to booked — 3 hrs
- [ ] Sweep every conversation: anyone warm gets 2 specific time slots offered, not "let me know."
- [ ] Send the offer one-pager (file 04) to all warm-but-hesitant.

### Day 9 — Sat, Jul 25: Breakup wave — 2 hrs
- [ ] "Closing the file" email to all non-repliers (file 04) — breakup emails routinely pull the highest reply rate of the sequence.

### Day 10 — Sun, Jul 26: Tally + prep — 2 hrs

- [ ] Prep each call: their reviews, their ads, their storm map, your 3 recommendations.
- [ ] Queue Monday's confirmations. Replies will keep trickling in all next week — the sprint keeps paying.

## Non-negotiables (the whole game)

1. **Reply to every response within 5 minutes** during work hours. You're selling speed-to-lead — demonstrate it.
2. **Always be booking.** Every touch drives to the calendar link or offers two time slots. Never end warm threads with a question that can be answered "maybe later."
3. **Log every touch** the moment it happens. What isn't tracked doesn't improve.
4. **3 hours minimum daily**, calls in owner-friendly windows (before 10am / after 4pm).
5. **Don't stop when you hit 5.** Book 8; some will no-show.

## On the discovery call (so leads become clients)

1. Diagnose, don't pitch, for the first 15 min: current lead flow, cost per job, what their last agency screwed up, response time to leads (call their own number live — if it rings out, that's the close).
2. Show the machine: 2-min screen share of the speed-to-lead workflow + a storm map of their metro.
3. Present the guarantee offer. Ask for the start date, not the sale.

## After the sprint (weeks 3–6)

- Warmed domains come online → scale to 100–150 emails/day via Instantly.
- Turn ads back on **for retargeting + the storm offer** using file 05's checklist.
- Publish 2 case studies (even free/beta clients count) — video testimonial + numbers.
- Systematize referrals: 10% of first 3 months for any referred client.
- Start the compounding channel: 1 YouTube/Reel per week showing real dashboards ("what a $47 roofing lead looks like").
