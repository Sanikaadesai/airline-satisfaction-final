# Why Your Ads Aren't Working — and the Fix

You said you're running ads without the results you wanted. Across contractor ad accounts, poor results almost always trace to a handful of the same causes — and most of them are *after the click*, not in the ad.

## 2026 benchmarks first (so you know what "good" looks like)

| Channel | Cost per roofing lead | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Google Local Services Ads (LSA) | $45–150 | Best ROI of any paid channel for most roofers; pay-per-lead, shows above everything |
| Meta/Facebook ads | $20–110 | Cheapest volume; leads are colder — speed-to-lead decides everything |
| Google Search ads | ~$187 avg; $100–500+ in hot metros | $50–100+ CPCs; mature accounts get $20–35/lead, new accounts pay tuition |
| Aggregators (Angi etc.) | ~$40 | Shared 4+ ways; close rates in single digits |
| **Blended average** | **~$105** | Judge channels on **cost per booked job**, not cost per lead |

The controlling metric: a $50 shared lead closing at 3% = $1,667/job. A $99 exclusive lead closing at 20% = $495/job. **Cheap leads are usually the expensive ones.**

## The 10-point diagnostic (run your account through this today)

1. **Campaign objective = Leads.** Not traffic, not engagement, not awareness. Anything else buys clicks that never convert.
2. **You quit during the learning phase.** The first 1–2 weeks run 2–3x higher CPL while the algorithm learns. Killing/restarting campaigns resets the meter — you keep paying tuition and never graduating.
3. **No qualifying friction on the form.** Use Meta's **Higher Intent** form type (adds a review step; cuts junk leads 20–30%) plus 2–3 qualifying questions (homeowner? roof age? insurance claim filed?).
4. **Speed-to-lead is broken (the #1 killer).** A Facebook lead is hot for ~5 minutes; after 30 minutes your connect rate drops ~80%, and 5-minute responders convert up to 400% better. If leads wait hours for a callback, the ads were fine — the follow-up wasn't. Install the 30-second auto-text (file 02) before spending another dollar.
5. **No retargeting layer.** Retargeting converts 3–5x better than cold. Minimum three audiences: site visitors (30d), 50%+ video viewers, form abandoners. This is the cheapest money in the account.
6. **Weak creative.** Before/after photos are the single best-performing format for home services; 15–30 second real-job Reels get 2–3x the reach of static images. Stock photos are poison — homeowners smell them instantly.
7. **Targeting too broad or too narrow.** Homeowners-only, exclude recent converters, metro-level geo. Let the algorithm work inside sane guardrails.
8. **No LSA while competitors own it.** If you're (or your clients are) not on Local Services Ads, the best-ROI channel is being handed to competitors.
9. **Landing page ≠ ad promise.** Storm ad → storm landing page with claim help, reviews, and a 2-field form. Generic homepage kills storm traffic. Pre-build the storm page *before* the next event — activation within 48h of a storm is where the 300–800% search spike gets captured.
10. **Measuring the wrong number.** Report cost per *booked inspection* weekly. It reframes every optimization conversation and it's how you'll prove SEA's value to clients.

## Strategy note for SEA itself

For **SEA's own client acquisition**, cold ads are the slow, expensive channel — B2B "roofers who want an agency" is a brutal cold-ads audience. During the 10-day sprint: outbound does the hunting; keep ~$10–20/day of retargeting on site visitors and video viewers so every prospect who Googles you sees proof everywhere. Scale ads for SEA only after you have 2–3 case studies to run as creative.

For **client campaigns**, this checklist *is* the audit you sell: run a prospect's account through it on the discovery call and the gaps close the deal for you.
