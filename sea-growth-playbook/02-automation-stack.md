# The Automation Stack — Run SEA On It, Then Sell It

Two jobs for one stack: (1) automate SEA's own lead gen so you're not doing everything manually, and (2) package the *same system* as your productized client offer. Agencies running this model routinely double client capacity without adding headcount.

## Core platform: GoHighLevel (GHL)

Agency unlimited plan ≈ $297/mo — white-label it as "SEA Growth System." One sub-account per client, one for SEA itself.

### The 7 workflows to build (in priority order)

| # | Workflow | Trigger | What it does | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **30-second speed-to-lead** | Any new lead (form, FB lead ad, GBP chat, missed call) | Instant SMS + email from the owner's name; AI qualifies (address, roof age, insurance claim?); notifies sales rep | Responding in 5 min converts up to **400% better** than 1 hour; a lead is "hot" for ~5 minutes |
| 2 | **Missed-call text-back** | Missed inbound call | Texts back within 60 seconds: "Sorry we missed you — is this about a roof repair or replacement?" | Roofers miss 30–40% of calls while on roofs; this alone often pays for the retainer |
| 3 | **7-day new-lead nurture** | Lead not booked after first contact | Day 1/2/4/7 SMS + email sequence with reviews, before/after photos, booking link | Most contractors give up after 1 attempt; cadenced follow-up doubles contact rates |
| 4 | **Unsold estimate chase** | Estimate sent, not signed in 48h | Automated 3-week chase (48h, 5d, 10d, 21d) with financing options and urgency | Recovers jobs already paid for — the biggest hidden revenue leak |
| 5 | **Review engine** | Job marked complete | SMS review request + 1 reminder; routes unhappy replies to owner privately | Reviews drive the Google Map Pack = free leads; this is the compounding channel |
| 6 | **Storm trigger** | Hail/wind event in service area (HailTrace / hail.org / NOAA reports) | Activates pre-built storm landing page + ad set + SMS blast to past customers/neighbors list within 48h | Searches spike **300–800%** within 48h of a storm; weather-triggered campaigns get 3.2x opens at 57% lower cost per lead |
| 7 | **Database reactivation** | Season start (spring/fall) or slow week | SMS to old leads/customers: free inspection offer | Cheapest leads you'll ever get — the list is already paid for |

Setup time for workflows 1–5: **3–5 hours** using a roofing snapshot template. Do SEA's own sub-account this weekend (it's in the 10-day plan).

## SEA's own outbound automation (for getting *clients*)

| Layer | Tool | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Prospect list | Google Maps scraping (Outscraper) + Apollo.io | Filter: roofers in storm-hit metros, 10–50 employees, has website, running or recently ran ads |
| Ad-spend signal | **Meta Ad Library** (free) | Roofers *already running ads* are proven marketing buyers — your hottest list |
| Cold email | Instantly / Smartlead | ⚠️ New domains need ~2 weeks warm-up. For this 10-day sprint, send manually from your main domain (20–30/day max) and lean on calls/DMs/Looms; start warming 2 secondary domains **today** for August scale |
| Personalization at scale | Clay (later) | For now: manual first lines referencing their reviews/storms/ads |
| Video audits | Loom (free tier) | The 34%-reply-rate weapon — checklist in file 04 |
| Booking | GHL calendar (or Calendly) | Every message links here — remove friction |
| Pipeline | GHL: New → Contacted → Replied → Call Booked → Proposal → Won | Log every touch; speed-to-lead applies to YOU too — answer replies within 5 minutes |

## The productized offer this stack lets you sell

> **"The Roofing Lead Machine" — $1,500–$2,500/mo + ad spend, 90-day initial term**
> 1. Meta + Google LSA campaigns (exclusive leads, never shared)
> 2. 30-second speed-to-lead automation + missed-call text-back installed on their number
> 3. 7-day nurture + estimate chase + review engine
> 4. Weekly report on **cost per booked inspection** (not vanity metrics)
> **Guarantee: 10 qualified leads in the first 30 days or the management fee is refunded/waived.**

The guarantee is safe: at Meta's $25–110 per roofing lead, 10 leads ≈ $250–$1,100 of their ad spend. You're guaranteeing something the benchmarks say is very likely, and it demolishes the "we got burned before" objection.

Optional entry-ladder (copy Roofing Webmasters): a $499 one-time "Speed-to-Lead Install" — foot in the door, upsell to the retainer after they see recovered calls in week one.
