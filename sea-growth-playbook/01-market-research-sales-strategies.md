# What the $100k–$500k/Month Companies Actually Do

You asked to study companies doing $100k–$500k. There are two groups worth copying, and SEA needs lessons from both:

- **A. Roofing-marketing agencies at that revenue level** (your competitors — copy how they *sell*)
- **B. Roofing companies at that revenue level** (your ideal clients — understand how they *buy* and grow, so your pitch matches)

---

## A. The agencies: who they are and how they win clients

|---|---|---|---|
| **Hook Agency** | Roofing + home services only; explicitly targets top 5% retail roofers with **$2M+ revenue floor** | SEO/content authority — they rank #1 for "roofing marketing agencies" and publish rankings of their own competitors; YouTube; speaking at trade shows | Premium retainers |

### The 7 sales strategies they share

1. **Hard niche positioning.** "We only do roofing." They speak the trade's language (squares, supplements, retail vs. storm, insurance claims) and show case studies with real job dollar amounts. Generalists lose to them every time.
2. **Content authority engine.** They rank for the exact phrases roofers Google when they're mad at their current agency ("best roofing marketing agency", "roofing leads cost"). They even publish "top 10 agencies" lists *featuring themselves*. Inbound compounds while outbound pays the bills.
3. **Productized offers with visible pricing.** Tiers, entry offers ($99/mo), transparent scope. The advice circulating in the industry is literally "avoid agencies with $5K+/month minimums until your revenue supports it" — agencies that publish fair tiers harvest everyone scared off by the big minimums.
4. **Risk reversal / performance framing.** Pay-per-lead options, exclusive (not shared) leads, and guarantee-backed offers. This is the #1 objection-killer for burned roofers — and almost every roofer has been burned by an agency before.
5. **Speed-to-lead automation as the product itself.** The winning agencies don't just run ads — they install the follow-up machine (GoHighLevel-type CRM, 30-second text-back, 7-day nurture). Ads + automation is the bundle; ads alone is a commodity.
6. **Storm-surge readiness.** They pre-build hail landing pages and campaigns and activate within 48 hours of a storm. Weather-triggered campaigns get 3.2x higher open rates and cost 57% less per lead than manually launched ones (ServiceTitan data).
7. **Community and partnerships.** Supplier relationships (ABC Supply, SRS, GAF/Owens Corning distributor networks), trade shows (IRE, Win the Storm), roofing Facebook groups, D2D/sales communities. Referrals from suppliers are warm and free.

### How their outbound actually performs (real benchmarks)

- Cold email to roofing/commercial-property niches gets **7–14% reply rates** when it's short, local, and anchored to a real signal (a storm event, permit history, building age) — not generic "we do marketing" blasts.
- **Multi-touch sequences (3–5 touches across email + LinkedIn + phone) double or triple reply rates** vs. one-shot emails.
- **Loom video audits** (2–3 minute screen recordings showing a prospect 2–3 specific things broken in their marketing) have produced **~34% reply rates** in documented agency case studies.
- Realistic timeline via direct outreach: **first client in 2–4 weeks.** That's why your 10-day/5-lead goal is aggressive but doable — leads (booked calls), not signed clients, in 10 days.

### The gap SEA can own

Hook targets $2M+ roofers. Roofing Webmasters bottom-fishes at $99/mo. **The underserved middle: roofers doing roughly $1M–$5M/year ($100k–$400k/month) who want to scale but can't get Hook's attention and need more than a $99 website plan.** That's your ICP. They have ad budget ($2k–$10k/mo), they answer their own phones, and the owner makes the buying decision in one or two calls.

## B. The roofing companies at $100k–$500k/month: how they grow (and buy)

Understanding this makes your pitch land, because you're selling them the playbook they already know they need:

1. **They stop owner-selling and build a sales team.** Owner-selling caps growth; more reps = more inspections = more closes. *Your angle: "your reps are only as good as the lead flow we feed them."*
2. **They respond to leads in minutes, not hours.** Calling a lead within 5 minutes is **21x more effective** than waiting 30 minutes. Storm-market roofers mobilize within 72 hours of an event. *Your angle: sell the speed-to-lead automation.*
3. **They run multi-channel, not one channel.** Google Business Profile + reviews + Local Services Ads + Search Ads + Meta retargeting, in coordination. *Your angle: "you're running one channel (that's why results stalled)."*
4. **They standardize follow-up cadences.** Every unsold estimate gets chased automatically for weeks. Persistent multi-channel follow-up is where the hidden revenue is. *Your angle: automation recovers deals they already paid to generate.*
5. **They treat storm season as 40–70% of annual revenue** in hail markets and prepare *before* the storm. *Your angle: June 1 / June 26 / July 7 storm metros are live right now.*
6. **They add high-margin adjacent services** (gutters, solar) to raise average ticket. *Your angle: bigger ticket = more room to pay for leads.*
7. **They measure cost per booked job, not cost per lead.** A $50 shared lead closing at 3% ($1,667/job) loses to a $99 exclusive lead closing at 20% ($495/job). *Your angle: report CPA in your dashboards — it's how sophisticated buyers judge you.*
