# Outreach Scripts — Copy, Personalize the First Line, Send

Rules: under 100 words per email. One specific signal in line 1. One CTA. No links in email #1 (deliverability). Plain text, from a person, not "SEA Marketing Team."

## Loom email/DM (for the top 50)

**Subject:** made you a 3-min video, {{FirstName}}

> Recorded a quick video walking through {{CompanyName}}'s lead flow — 3 things I'd fix, starting with {{most glaring: "your number rings out after hours" / "no LSA profile while {{competitor}} owns the map pack"}}. No pitch, just the walkthrough: {{loom_link}}
>
> If it's useful, I'll show you the fix on a 15-min call.

### Loom recording checklist (3 min max, don't polish)
1. Their Google Business Profile vs. top competitor (reviews, photos, posts)
2. Search "roofer {{city}}" — are they in the map pack / LSA? Who is?
3. Meta Ad Library — their ads (or absence) vs. a competitor spending
4. Call their number on screen, or submit their contact form → "I'll show you what happens next... nothing. That's the leak."
5. Close: "Three fixes, ~2 weeks to install, and we guarantee 10 leads in 30 days. Booking link below."

## Cold call script

**Opener (permission-based):**
> "{{FirstName}}? {{YourName}} with SEA — I'll be straight with you, this is a cold call, but it's about the hail that hit {{city}} — got 30 seconds, or should I call after 5?"

**The pitch (if yes):**
> "We run lead systems for roofers. Two things: exclusive ads in your metro, and an automation that texts every lead back in 30 seconds — because leads answered in 5 minutes close about 4x more. We back it with a guarantee: 10 qualified leads in your first 30 days or you don't pay us. I'm not asking you to buy anything today — just 15 minutes to show you what homeowners in {{city}} are searching right now. Does {{Tue 8:30am}} or {{Tue 4:30pm}} work?"

**Objections:**
- *"We're slammed."* → "Perfect — storm work dries up in 60–90 days. The roofers who set up lead flow now own the retail season after. 15 minutes, and if it's not useful I'll leave you alone."
- *"We tried an agency, got burned."* → "Half our clients say that. It's why the guarantee exists — 10 leads in 30 days or you don't pay. All the risk is mine."
- *"Send me info."* → "Will do — and I'll include a 3-minute video of your specific market so it's not generic fluff. If it makes sense, we talk 15 minutes. What's the best email?" *(Then actually send the Loom same day.)*
- *"How much?"* → "Depends on your market, but the ballpark is $1,500–$2,500 a month plus ad spend, exclusive leads, no long contract, and month one is guaranteed. That's exactly what the 15 minutes is for."

## Follow-up sequence (email; non-repliers)

**Bump #1 (Day +2):** "Bubbling this up, {{FirstName}} — worth 15 minutes? If not, tell me to buzz off, no hard feelings."

**Bump #2 (Day +5):** "One thing I didn't mention: the guarantee is in writing — 10 qualified leads in 30 days or the fee's $0. We can only run one roofer per metro, and I'd rather it be a company with {{CompanyName}}'s reviews. Tue or Thu?"

**Bump #3 / Breakup (Day +8):** "Closing your file, {{FirstName}} — I'll take the hint 🙂. If lead flow ever becomes the bottleneck, the door's open. Leaving you one thing: answer every lead within 5 minutes (auto-text if you can't) — it's worth more than most agencies charge for. Good luck with the storm season."

*(Breakup emails routinely out-reply every other touch. Send them.)*
