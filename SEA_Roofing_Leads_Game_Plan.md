# SEA (Sanika Elevation Agency) — Roofing Leads Game Plan
**Goal: 5 qualified leads (booked sales calls with roofing/home-improvement owners) in 10 days — July 17–27, 2026**

---

## Executive Summary

The agencies winning in the roofing niche don't win because their ads are better. They win because of four repeatable patterns:

1. **They niche hard and publish proof** — case studies with real client names and numbers, not "we do marketing."
2. **They sell a system, not clicks** — speed-to-lead automation, follow-up sequences, and review engines wrapped around the ads.
3. **They align pricing with results** — pay-per-lead, pay-per-appointment, or performance guarantees, because roofers have been burned by retainers.
4. **They rank/show up where roofers already look** — buyer-intent content, Facebook roofing groups, and manufacturer/CRM ecosystems.

Your fastest path to 5 leads in 10 days is **not ads** — it's direct outreach (calls + DMs + Loom audits) with a storm-season, performance-based offer, backed by an automated follow-up system. Ads are the slowest channel at your stage; outbound is the fastest. This doc gives you the full plan.

## Part 2 — The Market Math (your sales ammo)

Use these numbers in every pitch. Roofers respect people who know their economics.

**Lead costs (2026 benchmarks):**
- Qualified roofing lead: **$80–$220** (over $300 in competitive metros)
- Google LSA: **$20–$80/lead** ($80–$150 in storm-prone metros) — highest intent
- Google Ads non-branded search: ~**$124 CPL**; Performance Max: ~**$64 CPL**
- Facebook lead ads: ~**$5.83 CPL** raw — cheap but low-intent, only works with instant follow-up
- Shared leads (Angi/Thumbtack): $40–$90 but sold to 3–5 competitors → 5–15% close → **$133–$400 per booked job**
- Exclusive leads retail for **$150–$250**

**Speed-to-lead (the #1 conversion lever):**
- Contacting a lead within **5 minutes** increases conversion up to **900%** vs. waiting an hour
- Winning campaigns run a **3-touch sequence (call + SMS + email) within 15 minutes** of form submission
- Industry-average close rate: **27%**. Top performers with systematic follow-up: **35–45%**
- Homeowners contact multiple roofers; **the first responder almost always wins the callback**

**The gap you sell into:**
- **81% of roofing companies have no systematic review generation process** (while 98% of consumers read reviews before contacting a local business)
- Most roofers under $1M are owner-operators on a roof all day — they physically cannot answer calls in 5 minutes. That's the problem SEA's automation solves.

**Right now (July 2026) is storm season:**
- July 3–4 hail confirmed in Duluth GA, Dacula GA, Acworth GA, and the St. Cloud/Waite Park MN metro
- TX/OK are having one of the most active severe-weather seasons on record; insurance claim volumes are ~2x historical average and adjusters are stretched thin
- Many homeowner policies require storm claims within 12 months → built-in urgency for homeowners AND roofers

## Part 3 — The Offer (what you sell)

Roofers have been burned by $2,000–$5,000/mo retainers with no results. The research is clear: they want **incentive alignment** and **capacity-paced leads**. So sell a pilot they can't compare to a retainer:

### The Storm-Season Pilot (recommended)
> "We'll book **10 storm-inspection appointments onto your calendar in 30 days**, exclusive to you, with every lead called/texted within 5 minutes automatically. Flat pilot fee + you only continue if it works. If we don't hit the number, we work free until we do."

- Price the pilot at **$1,000–$1,500 setup + $75–$150 per booked appointment** (undercuts exclusive-lead retail at $150–$250 while your FB CPL is ~$6–$30)
- Ad spend is theirs (~$1,000–$1,500/mo) — keeps your risk near zero and filters serious buyers
- Cap it: "We take 1 roofer per service area" (exclusivity is a real closer in this niche)

### Why this closes
- Performance-priced → removes the retainer objection
- Appointments, not "leads" → speaks to what they actually want
- Storm angle → timely, urgent, seasonal
- Exclusivity → scarcity that's actually true

## Part 4 — The Automation Stack (how you deliver + scale)

Build this once as a GoHighLevel (or similar) snapshot; deploy per client in under a day. This is the "system" that justifies your price and retains clients:

1. **Speed-to-lead engine** — new lead triggers: instant SMS (within ~15 seconds) + auto-dial bridge to the roofer + email; 3 touches inside 15 minutes
2. **Missed-call text-back** — every missed call to the roofer gets an instant SMS before the homeowner dials a competitor
3. **AI voice agent** — answers 24/7, qualifies (storm damage? insurance? address?), books inspections straight onto the calendar; GHL supports re-billing the "AI Employee" for recurring margin
4. **Nurture + no-show reactivation** — 7-day and 30-day sequences for leads that don't book / don't show
5. **Database reactivation** — blast the roofer's old quote list ("storm season inspection — you had hail in your area") = fastest first win, often produces appointments in week 1 with $0 ad spend
6. **Review engine** — SMS review request within 24h of job completion (fixes the 81% gap; 10+ new reviews/month is the benchmark)
7. **For SEA internally** — run the SAME stack on your own pipeline: every prospect gets instant follow-up, sequences, and pipeline stages. You are your own first case study.

## Part 5 — Why Your Current Ads Aren't Working (fix checklist)

Run every campaign (yours and clients') through this:

- [ ] **Dedicated landing page per campaign** — ad about storm damage → page about storm damage (generic homepage = conversion killer)
- [ ] **One offer, one CTA** — free storm inspection with a form + phone number, nothing else
- [ ] **Follow-up inside 5 minutes** — if leads wait an hour, the ads were never the problem
- [ ] **Storm/seasonal creative** — real local photos, real crews; targeting built around storm-hit zips, not broad interest audiences
- [ ] **Benchmark honestly** — lead-gen campaigns average ~7.7–9% conversion; FB roofing CPL should land ~$6–$30, $30–$60 per booked appointment with fast follow-up; a 33% lead→appointment rate is above average
- [ ] **Skip broad Facebook prospecting** — the channels that consistently win for roofers in 2026: Google Business Profile + storm SEO, LSAs, insurance/adjuster referral partners, and reviews

**Strategic note:** for signing *agency clients*, ads are your slowest channel. Outbound + community + proof is how agencies at your stage sign their first 10–20 clients. Ads on your own agency come later, funded by retainer revenue.

## Part 6 — The 10-Day Sprint (July 17–27): 5 Leads

**Definition:** 1 lead = 1 booked sales call with a roofing/home-improvement owner-operator ($100k–$1M revenue, runs some ads or buys shared leads, in/near a storm-hit market).

**Channel choice for 10 days:** phone + Facebook/Instagram DM + Loom audits + referrals. **Do NOT launch mass cold email** — new sending domains need 2–3 weeks of warm-up; without it you land in spam and burn your domain. Low-volume (≤20/day) personalized email from your existing warm inbox is fine.

### Build the list (Day 1–2) — target 150–200 prospects
- **GAF certified contractor directory** (gaf.com/en-us/roofing-contractors) — filter by storm-hit states: GA, MN, TX, OK
- **Google Maps**: "roofing contractor" in Duluth GA, Dacula GA, Acworth GA, St. Cloud MN, DFW TX, OKC — prioritize companies with 10–80 reviews (big enough to pay, small enough to need you)
- **Angi/Thumbtack/HomeAdvisor listings** — these roofers already PAY for shared leads; they're pre-qualified buyers hurting from 5–15% close rates
- **Facebook roofing groups** — Roofing Insights Private Group, The Roofing Support Group, Roofers Helping Roofers, The Roofing & Solar Community (join Day 1; groups often take 1–2 days to approve)
- Optional paid accelerant: **Shovels.ai** permit data — pull contractors actively filing roofing permits (most active = best prospects)
- Track everything in one CRM/sheet: company, owner name, phone, FB profile, reviews count, storm market Y/N, ads running Y/N (check Meta Ad Library)

### Daily operating rhythm (Days 2–10, ~3–4 focused hours/day)
| Activity | Daily target | Expected yield over sprint |
|---|---|---|
| Cold calls to owners (8–10am or 4–6pm — they're off the roof) | 30–40 dials | ~250 dials → 25–40 conversations → 3–5 calls booked |
| Personalized FB/IG DMs | 20 | ~160 DMs → 15–25 replies → 2–4 calls booked |
| Loom video audits (3 min, for warm/replied prospects) | 3–5 | ~30 Looms → 2–5x reply lift on those threads |
| Group participation (answer questions, zero pitching) | 30 min | Inbound DMs + credibility by Day 5+ |
| Follow-ups (every prior touch, 48h cadence) | all due | Most bookings come from touch 2–4, not touch 1 |

### Day-by-day
- **Day 1 (Fri Jul 17):** Build list (100+). Join FB groups. Set up tracking sheet/CRM + calendar booking link. Draft scripts (below). Fix your own site's above-the-fold: "We book storm-inspection appointments for roofers. Pay per appointment."
- **Day 2 (Sat):** Finish list to 150–200. First 20 dials + 20 DMs (Saturday morning is good — owners aren't on roofs). Record 3 Looms for the best-fit prospects.
- **Day 3 (Sun):** Light day: 20 DMs, prep Monday call blocks, post first helpful answer in groups.
- **Days 4–8 (Mon–Fri):** Full rhythm daily (30–40 dials, 20 DMs, 3–5 Looms, follow-ups). Every reply gets a response within 5 minutes — you are selling speed-to-lead; demonstrate it.
- **Day 9–10 (Sat–Sun Jul 26–27):** Follow-up blitz on every open thread + final call block. Count: booked calls should be at 5–8.

### Math check
250 dials + 160 DMs + 30 Looms with disciplined follow-up reliably produces **5–10 booked calls** at typical reply/booking rates (cold outreach benchmarks: 5–6% reply, 1–2% meetings; personalized DM + Loom outperforms this). The plan has margin — 5 is the floor if you do the volume. **The volume is the strategy.** If by Day 6 you're under 2 bookings: double dial volume, and add 10 local drop-ins (supply houses like ABC Supply/SRS at 6:30–8am, where roofers pick up materials).

## Part 7 — Scripts

### Cold call opener (30 seconds)
> "Hey [Name], this is [You] with SEA — I'll be quick. We work only with roofers, and with the hail that just hit [their metro], we're booking storm-inspection appointments for one roofer per area — you only pay per appointment that lands on your calendar, and every lead gets called and texted within 5 minutes automatically. Do you have capacity for more inspection appointments in the next 30 days, or are you full?"

("Are you full?" flips the frame — nobody says they're full.)

- Objection "I've been burned by marketers": "That's exactly why we don't do retainers. You pay per appointment booked on your calendar. If we book nothing, you pay nothing past setup. Worst case, you got a free system installed."
- Close: "Let's do 20 minutes tomorrow — I'll show you the exact system and the numbers from your market. Morning or evening better?"

### DM (Facebook/Instagram)
> "Hey [Name] — saw [Company] serves [City]. Quick one: after the hail on July 4th, are you set up so every storm lead that calls or fills a form gets a response within 5 minutes? Asking because that one thing is worth ~9x on conversions, and most crews can't do it from a roof. I made a 3-min video on how [Company] specifically could — want me to send it?"

(Permission-first — record the Loom only after they say yes. You only make videos for warm prospects.)

### Loom audit structure (3 minutes)
1. Their Google Business Profile + reviews vs. the top competitor in their city (30s)
2. Test their own lead response: "I filled your form / called Tuesday 2pm — here's what happened" (60s) — this is the killer moment, most fail
3. The fix: speed-to-lead + missed-call text-back + review engine, and the pay-per-appointment pilot (60s)
4. CTA: booking link (15s)

### Follow-up cadence per prospect
Touch 1 (call/DM) → +48h: SMS/DM bump → +48h: Loom or value drop (their market's storm data) → +72h: "closing the loop" message → into 30-day nurture. Never one-and-done; touches 2–4 book most of the calls.

## Part 8 — After the Sprint: The Compounding Engine (Days 11–90)

1. **Close 2–3 of the 5 calls** into pilots; over-deliver; document everything (screenshots of booked appointments, CPL, response times)
2. **Turn pilot results into your proof engine** — one written case study + one video testimonial per client; publish everywhere (this is the Hook Agency / Profit Roofing Systems move)
3. **Warm up cold-email infrastructure NOW** (secondary domain, 2–3 mailboxes, 2–3 week warm-up) so scaled outbound is ready in August — peak storm-restoration season continues
4. **Referral loops:** ask every client for 1 intro to a non-competing roofer (different metro) with a real incentive (one month of free appointments); partner with adjusters, supply houses, and roofing CRM communities
5. **Buyer-intent content:** publish your market's numbers ("What roofing leads cost in [State] in 2026") — the same transparent-benchmark play that gets agencies like Biddable and Hook found by roofers on Google
6. **Productize the stack** — the GHL snapshot means each new client is <1 day of setup; sell the AI voice agent as a re-billed add-on for margin
7. **Then** turn ads back on for SEA itself — retargeting your content viewers first (cheapest, warmest), cold audiences later
