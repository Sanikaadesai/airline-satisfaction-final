# SEA (Sanika Elevation Agency) — Roofing Leads Game Plan

**Goal: 5 qualified leads (booked sales calls with roofing / home-improvement owners) between Fri, July 17 and Sun, July 26, 2026.**

"Lead" here means a *booked, held appointment* with a decision-maker at a roofing or home-improvement company — not a form fill. To hold 5, we aim to book 8–10 (expect ~30–40% no-shows).

---

## 1. What the $100k–$500k/month agencies in this niche actually do

Researched across home-services agency case studies, cold-outreach benchmark studies, and the GoHighLevel agency ecosystem. Five patterns repeat at every agency in this revenue band:

### a) They sell an outcome with a guarantee — not "marketing services"
- They never sell "Facebook ads" or "SEO." They sell **"X booked roof-replacement estimates in 30/90 days — or you don't pay our fee."**
- The dominant model at scale is **pay-per-lead / pay-per-appointment**: one documented pay-per-lead agency runs multi-millions with just **7 clients**, and operators report **$100k+/mo from a single client** ([Flexxable](https://flexxable.com/the-recession-proof-client-landing-guide-to-100k-per-month/)). Clients pay only for leads delivered — no retainer trust-barrier to break.
- GHL-agency pricing guidance says the same: don't sell "CRM setup for $97/mo," sell "automated appointment booking + follow-up that produces 30% more booked jobs" ([NetPartners](https://netpartners.marketing/gohighlevel-agency-pricing-guide/)).
- A "micro-agency" doing **$127k/mo at 73% margin** does it with 1–2 contractors and a tight niche offer, not headcount ([Marshall Hargrave](https://marshallhargrave.medium.com/the-100k-mo-micro-agency-model-nobody-talks-about-de3ad4e862c4)).

### b) They get *their own* clients with outbound, not ads
- Ads to acquire agency clients is the most expensive channel there is at the $0–50k/mo stage. The agencies that scaled did it on **cold email + cold calls + DMs + partnerships**, then layered ads back in once case studies existed.
- Cold email to the roofing segment gets **7–14% reply rates** when done right ([LeadHaste](https://leadhaste.com/blog/cold-email-template-for-roofing)); one roofing-industry outbound program booked **167 qualified appointments / $700k+ pipeline in a few months** ([Abstrakt](https://www.abstraktmg.com/best-cold-email-agencies/)).
- What makes it work: emails under 100 words, referencing a **specific signal** (a storm, a permit, a missed call), asking for a **small commitment** (15-min call), with **3–5 follow-up touches minimum** — the first follow-up alone adds 40–50% more replies ([Instantly](https://instantly.ai/blog/cold-email-reply-rate-benchmarks/), [Martal](https://martal.ca/b2b-cold-email-statistics-lb/)).
- **Owners/founders are the highest-replying seniority group** in cold outreach, and sub-50-employee companies reply the most ([Belkins](https://belkins.io/blog/cold-email-response-rates)) — perfect, because that's exactly who runs a $1–5M roofing company.

### c) They fish where roofers already gather
- **Facebook groups**: Roofing Business Owners Only (Roofing Insights), The Roofing Support Group, Roofers Helping Roofers, The Roofing & Solar Community, Roofing Masters Network ([JobNimbus](https://www.jobnimbus.com/blog/the-7-best-roofing-groups-to-join-on-facebook), [Hook Agency](https://hookagency.com/blog/best-roofing-facebook-groups/)). Hook Agency — one of the biggest roofing-niche agencies — literally grew by being helpful in these groups.
- **Supply houses & distributors**: SRS Distribution, ABC Supply, Beacon, QXO branches are where every local roofer stands at a counter 2–3 mornings a week. Branch managers know everyone and love vendors who help their contractors sell more ([SRS](https://www.srsdistribution.com/en/news-and-events/contractor-solutions/the-power-of-networking/)).
- **Associations & trade shows**: state roofing associations, International Roofing Expo, MRCA, Western Roofing Expo ([NewTech](https://newtechmachinery.com/learning-center/best-national-and-regional-roofing-associations/), [ProLine](https://useproline.com/roofing-trade-shows-and-conferences-2026/)), plus RoofersCoffeeShop online.

### d) Their backend is GoHighLevel + speed-to-lead automation
- The standard stack in this niche: **GHL sub-account per client** (pipelines, missed-call text-back, review automation, booking calendars) + Google/Facebook ads feeding it ([GHL roofing playbook](https://www.gohighlevel.com/roofing-playbook), [GHL for roofing](https://www.gohighlevel.ai/blog/gohighlevel-for-roofing)).
- Speed-to-lead is the whole ballgame — and the best sales weapon you have (see §3).

### e) They weaponize proof
- One case study ("we got Roofer X 22 estimates in 30 days at $85/lead") outperforms any funnel. Everything they publish is before/after numbers, screen-recorded dashboards, and client video clips — recycled into cold email, DMs, group posts, and retargeting ads.

## 2. Why your current ads probably aren't working (diagnosis checklist)

Run through this before spending another dollar:

| Check | The problem if it fails | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| Who are the ads targeting? | Ads *to roofers* (B2B) on Meta are low-trust and expensive at your stage — agencies are pitched daily. Outbound is 5–10x cheaper per booked call right now. | — |
| What's the offer in the ad? | "We do marketing for roofers" ≠ offer. A guarantee ("10 booked estimates in 30 days or free") or a free named audit is an offer. | — |
| Speed-to-lead on YOUR leads | If a form fill waits >5 min, you lose. 5-min response = **21x** more qualified conversations; **78%** of buyers pick the first responder; 2-min responders convert **62%** vs. the industry-average 28% at 42 min ([GreetNow](https://greetnow.com/blog/speed-to-lead-statistics), [PipelineOn](https://pipelineon.com/blog/responding-to-leads-faster-home-service/)) | <5 min, ideally <60 sec |
| Budget concentration | Under ~$50/day spread across cold audiences = the algorithm never exits learning. Concentrate or cut. | — |
| Retargeting running? | Warm traffic (site visitors, video viewers, engagers) is the only audience where small ad budgets pay at your stage. | — |
| If ads are for *clients* (roofing leads): CPL sanity | Roofing Google Ads CPL averages **$124–$126** ([SearchLight](https://searchlightdigital.io/roofing-google-ads-cost-per-lead/)); LSA & Facebook run **$20–$80** ([ContractorMarketingPros](https://contractormarketingpros.net/blog/the-cost-of-roofing-leads/)); exclusive leads book at **35–40%** vs 8–15% for shared ([GetBiddable](https://getbiddable.com/blog/roofing-lead-cost-benchmarks-2026)). Judge on **cost per booked job**, not CPL. | FB $20–80, Search ~$125, LSA $20–80 |

**Decision for the next 10 days: pause cold-audience ads (keep only retargeting if you have pixel traffic). Put 100% of effort into outbound + partnerships below.** Ads come back once you have a case-study asset to point them at.

## 3. Your July unfair advantage: the "missed call" pitch

It's July 17 — peak roofing season *and* peak hail season. Storm demand is surging: **22% of roof replacements are storm-driven and 12M+ US properties took hail damage in 2024** ([ToolBeltData](http://toolbeltdata.com/blog/roofing-market-trends-storm-demand-contractor-activity-data/)). Every roofer is on a roof, phone in pocket, missing calls.

Meanwhile only **12% of contractors respond to leads within 5 minutes**, and companies without after-hours automation lose **~34% of leads** — roughly **$140k/year for a business doing 80 leads/mo** ([GreetNow](https://greetnow.com/blog/lead-response-time-statistics), [US Tech Automations](https://ustechautomations.com/resources/blog/home-services-lead-response-speed-how-to-2026)).

**So don't pitch "marketing." Pitch the leak:**

> **The Secret-Shopper Audit (your #1 opener):** Call the roofer's own number midday like a homeowner. Record what happens (rings out? voicemail? 40-second hold?). Then send: *"I called your office Tuesday at 1pm as a homeowner with hail damage — it went to voicemail. 78% of homeowners book with whoever answers first. We fix that in 48 hours with missed-call text-back + an AI receptionist, and it pays for itself with one saved roof. Want the 2-minute recording?"*

That message references a specific, verifiable, painful fact about *their* business. It's the highest-reply cold message you can send, and the fix (GHL missed-call text-back + AI voice agent) is a productized service you deploy in a day — the foot in the door for the full lead-gen retainer.

## 4. The automation stack (build once, runs forever)

| Layer | Tool | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| List building | Outscraper / PhantomBuster (Google Maps), Apollo.io, state contractor license boards, county permit records | Pull every roofer in target metros: name, owner, phone, email, review count, website |
| Storm targeting | Interactive Hail Maps / HailTrace | Prioritize roofers in zips hit by storms in the last 60 days — they have insurance money in their market and capacity pain |
| Enrichment & verification | Apollo, Hunter, MillionVerifier | Owner mobile numbers + verified emails (keeps bounce rate <3%) |
| Cold email | Instantly.ai or Smartlead | 2–3 secondary domains, 2–3 inboxes each, 20–30 sends/inbox/day. **Buy domains TODAY — warmup takes ~2 weeks**, so at-scale email is your week-3+ channel, not this sprint's |
| Dialer | PhoneBurner / JustCall / OpenPhone | 50–100 dials/day without friction |
| CRM + nurture + booking | **GoHighLevel** (Agency plan) | One pipeline for SEA's own sales; snapshots you resell to clients; missed-call text-back; calendar |
| AI receptionist | GHL Voice AI / Retell / Bland | Answers client (and your) missed calls in seconds — most callers can't tell it's AI ([GetNextPhone](https://www.getnextphone.com/blog/speed-to-lead)); this IS the productized offer |
| Personalization at scale | Loom + Claude/ChatGPT | 3-min screen-recorded audits of a roofer's Google presence; AI drafts the first-line personalization from their reviews/site |

Total cost to stand this up: roughly $400–600/mo. One client retainer covers it 3–5x.

## 5. The math for 5 held leads in 10 days

Benchmarks: cold email books meetings at 0.5–2.5% of contacts ([Reachoutly](https://reachoutly.com/cold-email/conversion-rate/)); roofing-niche replies 7–14%; owners answer their phones in this trade; LinkedIn connects at ~27% / replies ~11% ([Outreaches](https://outreaches.ai/blog/cold-outreach-benchmarks)). Working multi-channel:

| Channel | Volume over 10 days | Expected booked calls |
|---|---|---|
| Cold calls (the workhorse — roofers answer phones) | 50–80 dials/day × 8 working days ≈ 500 dials → ~15% reach a decision-maker ≈ 75 convos | **4–7** |
| Secret-shopper audits + personalized email/Loom (existing inbox, 20–30/day) | ~200 sends | **1–3** |
| FB group value posts + 20 DMs/day | ~160 DMs | **1–2** |
| Supply-house visits + referral asks | 4–6 branch visits, every past contact asked | **1–2** |
| **Total booked** | | **7–14 → 5+ held** ✅ |

The plan works at *conservative* rates. Volume is the variable you control.

## 6. The 10-day sprint calendar (Jul 17–26)

**Day 1 — Fri Jul 17 (today): Build the machine**
- Write the one-sentence offer: *"We book hail/roof-replacement estimates for [metro] roofers — 10 booked estimates in your first 30 days or you don't pay our fee."*
- Scrape 300–500 roofers in 2–3 target metros (Outscraper + Apollo). Columns: owner, mobile, email, reviews, website, recent-storm zip (HailTrace).
- Buy 2–3 secondary domains, create inboxes, start Instantly/Smartlead warmup (ready ~Aug 1).
- Set up GHL pipeline + booking calendar. Pause cold-audience ads; leave retargeting.

**Day 2 — Sat Jul 18: Ammunition**
- Record 10 secret-shopper calls to your top-50 list (roofers answer Saturdays — they're at supply houses at 7am).
- Build the 1-page case study / proof asset (any past client result; if none, use the audit data itself: "we called 50 roofers; 38 missed the call").
- Draft scripts (below). 20 dials to Saturday-active roofers.

**Day 3 — Sun Jul 19: Light day**
- Join the 5 FB groups. No pitching — comment helpfully on 10 threads.
- Queue Monday's 80-call list, personalized first lines drafted with AI.

**Days 4–8 — Mon Jul 20 → Fri Jul 24: The grind (identical daily blocks)**
- **7:00–8:30a** — 20 dials (catch owners before they're on roofs; supply-house hours).
- **9:00–12:00** — 40 more dials. Voicemail + instant text on every miss (use your own missed-call automation — practice what you sell).
- **1:00–2:30p** — 5 secret-shopper audits → 5 Loom/email sends; 20–30 personalized emails.
- **3:00–4:00p** — 20 FB/IG DMs to group members + 1 value post every other day (post your audit stats — these go semi-viral in roofer groups).
- **4:00–5:00p** — Follow-ups (every prospect gets touch #2 within 48h; 3–5 touches minimum), CRM hygiene, book calls.
- **Tue or Thu morning**: visit 2 supply-house branches (ABC/SRS/Beacon). Bring coffee + donuts for the counter, one-pager for the branch manager: "I make your contractors sell more shingles — send me your busiest roofer who's missing calls; I'll fix it free and show you both."

**Day 9 — Sat Jul 25:** 20 early dials, all follow-ups current, second FB value post with week's audit data.

**Day 10 — Sun Jul 26:** Scorecard: dials, connects, replies, booked, held. Anything <5 held → double Monday's call block and re-run; the channels are proven, only volume moves.

## 7. Scripts

**Cold call (15 seconds):**
> "Hey [Name] — [You] from SEA, I'll be quick. I called your office yesterday posing as a homeowner with hail damage and it rang out to voicemail. I help [metro] roofers catch those calls and turn them into booked estimates — guaranteed 10 booked estimates in 30 days or you don't pay me. Worth 15 minutes this week to hear how?"

**Cold email #1 (secret-shopper, <100 words):**
> Subject: called your office Tuesday 1:14pm
>
> [Name] — I called [Company] Tuesday at 1:14pm as a homeowner with a hail claim. Voicemail.
>
> 78% of homeowners book with the first company that answers, and roofers without after-hours coverage leak ~34% of their leads — peak storm season makes it worse.
>
> We install missed-call text-back + an AI receptionist in 48 hours, then book estimates on top of it. 10 booked estimates in 30 days or you don't pay our fee.
>
> Want the 2-min recording of the call?

**Cold email #2 (follow-up, day 3):**
> Still have that recording. Also put together a 3-minute video of the 2 things on your Google profile costing you jobs vs [local competitor]. Send it over?

**FB/IG DM:**
> "Hey [Name], saw your post in [group] about [topic]. We ran a test this week — called 50 [state] roofers as a fake homeowner; 38 went to voicemail. Made a short breakdown of what the top 12% do differently. Want me to send it?"

**Referral ask (past clients / network):**
> "We're taking on 3 more [metro] roofing companies this month with a 10-estimates-in-30-days-or-free guarantee. Who's the one roofer you know who's slammed with storm work but terrible at answering their phone? An intro gets you [$500 / a free month]."

## 9. After the sprint — the repeatable machine ("how to get more, forever")

1. **Weeks 3–4:** warmed email domains come online → scale Instantly to 60–90 sends/day; keep 60 dials/day. Outbound becomes an always-on engine (~1,500 touches/mo → 8–15 booked calls/mo).
2. **First 1–2 clients:** deliver hard (their leads get <60-sec AI answer + text-back), screen-record everything → your first real case study within 30 days.
3. **Restart ads — but only retargeting + case-study creative**, pointed at a VSL/case-study page with a calendar. Cold ads only after the case study exists.
4. **Productize the ladder:** $0 audit → ~$500–997 setup "Never Miss a Call" system (48-hr install, instant value) → $1.5–3k/mo + per-lead lead-gen retainer with the 30-day guarantee → white-labeled GHL SaaS fee. Each rung pays for the next conversation.
5. **Partnership flywheel:** 1 supply-house branch + 1 state association relationship per month; sponsor a local roofing meetup for $200 and be the only marketer in the room.
6. **Content flywheel:** weekly post of real numbers (audits, CPLs, booked-estimate screenshots) in the 5 FB groups + LinkedIn — inbound starts as outbound's echo.
